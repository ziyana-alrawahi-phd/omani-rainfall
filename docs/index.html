Evaluating gridded rainfall against published station climatology

By Dr. Ziyana Al-Rawahi

This research portal evaluates how well NASA POWER reproduces the spatial rainfall climatology reported by Kwarteng et al. (2009), identifies systematic bias, and provides station- and region-level evidence for closer inspection.

Research objective

The central question is whether a gridded rainfall product can reproduce the broad station-based climatology of Oman sufficiently well to support subsequent spatial and temporal analysis.

Scientific scope

ImportantComparison period and interpretation boundary

The validation compares NASA POWER rainfall climatology for 1981–2003 with the station climatology published by Kwarteng et al. (2009) for 1977–2003. The periods are similar but not identical.

The extended NASA POWER record after 2003 is evaluated separately. This portal does not use the validation results to infer post-2003 rainfall trends.

Reference

Kwarteng, A. Y., Dorvlo, A. S. & Vijaya Kumar, G. T. (2009).
Analysis of a 27-year rainfall data (1977–2003) in the Sultanate of Oman.
